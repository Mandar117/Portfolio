ML Engineer — Leeds School of Business (Jan 2025 – Present)

Building transformer-based document classification systems for large-scale business text at CU Boulder's Leeds School of Business. Architected hierarchical RoBERTa and Longformer pipelines with custom preprocessing to handle documents far exceeding standard token limits, achieving strong F1 under heavy class imbalance.

Role: Machine Learning Engineer
Department: Business Analytics & Information Systems
Tools: Python, HuggingFace Transformers, RoBERTa, Longformer, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Pandas
Concepts: Long-Document NLP, Transformer Fine-tuning, Class Imbalance Handling, Hierarchical Modeling, Distributed Training, Deployment
Project Brief
Situation

Leeds School of Business researchers needed to classify large volumes of long-form business documents, but standard transformer models like BERT truncated content beyond 512 tokens — discarding crucial information mid-document.

Task

Engineer a scalable NLP classification pipeline capable of processing documents far exceeding standard token limits, maintaining high F1 performance across categories with severe class imbalance.

Action

Architected hierarchical RoBERTa and Longformer pipelines with custom chunking and sliding-window strategies, implemented class-weighted loss and oversampling to address imbalance, and fine-tuned models via HuggingFace Transformers and PyTorch on the curated business document corpus.

Result

Produced a deployed classification system achieving strong F1 across all document categories, enabling automated large-scale text analysis and significantly accelerating the school's business analytics research pipeline.

ML & Computer Vision Researcher — Rajiv Gandhi Science & Tech Commission (Jun 2023 – Jul 2024)

Designed and deployed a real-time Indian Sign Language recognition system using MediaPipe keypoint extraction and LSTM sequence modeling, achieving 97% test accuracy. Optimized the inference pipeline for a 20% speed improvement and published findings at an international Springer conference.

Role: ML & Computer Vision Researcher
Department: Applied Research Division
Tools: Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV, MediaPipe, LSTM, Keras
Concepts: Sequence Modeling, Gesture Recognition, Real-time Inference, Model Optimization, Academic Publication
Project Brief
Situation

India's ~18 million hearing-impaired citizens lacked access to a real-time, low-cost Indian Sign Language recognition tool — existing academic systems required expensive depth sensors or massive annotated datasets.

Task

Design, train, and deploy a real-time ISL recognition system operable on standard webcam hardware, targeting research-grade accuracy across 50+ gestures and suitable for international publication.

Action

Extracted hand and body keypoints via MediaPipe, trained LSTM sequence models on keypoint time-series, and optimized the inference pipeline through lightweight post-processing and quantization — reducing latency without sacrificing accuracy.

Result

Achieved 97% test accuracy with a 20% improvement in inference speed, publishing findings in international Springer conference proceedings and delivering a deployable real-time ISL recognition tool accessible without specialized hardware.
